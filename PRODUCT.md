## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Student & Faculty Innovators**: Engineering students, researchers, and faculty members at MAMCET and regional colleges in Tamil Nadu seeking to translate academic research and capstone projects into viable technology startups.
2. **Early-Stage Deep-Tech & Hardware Founders**: Innovators requiring physical prototyping infrastructure, 3D printing, testing instrumentation, and co-working facilities.
3. **Institutional Mentors & Ecosystem Partners**: Corporate advisors, academic experts, angel investors, StartupTN officers, and MSME coordinators evaluating, mentoring, and funding cohort ventures.

## Product Purpose

MASTeR Incubation Foundation (MIF) is the official digital portal and technology incubator platform of M.A.M. College of Engineering and Technology (MAMCET), Siruganur, Trichy. Its purpose is to guide entrepreneurs from ideation to scale through state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, statutory incorporation, structured mentoring, and non-dilutive seed grant enablement (including TANSEED and MSME schemes). Success means accelerating viable student and regional startups, de-risking deep-tech prototyping, and fostering an inclusive entrepreneurship ecosystem in Central Tamil Nadu.

## Positioning

Unlike generic co-working hubs or web-only startup directories, MASTeR Incubation Foundation provides an integrated institutional pipeline combining accredited university fabrication laboratories (MAMCET), statutory recognition (Section 8 Not-For-Profit Company), apex government affiliations (StartupTN Pre-Incubation Centre, MSME Recognized Host Institution), and national incubation alliances (IIT Madras Incubation Cell).

## Operating Context

- **Geographic & Institutional Base**: Trichy–Chennai Trunk Road, Siruganur, Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu – 621105.
- **Academic & Regulatory Anchors**: Anna University affiliated, AICTE approved, NBA accredited, IIC rated.
- **Government Portals & Schemes**: Direct linkage to StartupTN (EDII-TN), TANSEED ₹10 Lakhs seed fund grants, MSME Idea Hackathon funding up to ₹15 Lakhs, and patent facilitation.
- **Usage Modes**: Desktop and mobile web browsing by applicants, student teams submitting incubation proposals, investors discovering portfolio ventures, and attendees booking workshops.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Single-Page & Multipage Architecture**: Rich, interactive homepage with dedicated subpages (`/impact`, `/join-us`, `/about`, `/focus-areas`, `/programs`, `/facilities`, `/startups`, `/events`, `/contact`, `/apply`).
- **Interactive Persona Navigator**: Instant filtering for founders, researchers, students, and industry collaborators.
- **AI Incubation Diagnostic Copilot**: Built-in algorithmic evaluation tool helping innovators benchmark Technology Readiness Level (TRL 1–9) and structure grant applications.
- **Authentic Photographic Verification**: All showcased facilities, coordinators, and workspaces use verified on-campus photography.
- **No Fabricated Data**: Factual historical claims, real leadership (Incubation Coordinator Dr. Hemalatha), and accredited statutory affiliations only.

## Product Principles

1. **Authenticity Over Hype**: Every metric, facility, and affiliation must reflect verifiable institutional reality.
2. **Clear Institutional Hierarchy**: High-contrast, clean light theme with legible typography; avoid decorative visual noise.
3. **Action-Oriented Founder Pathways**: Every section leads to a frictionless next step (Apply, Explore Labs, Consult AI Copilot, Speak to Coordinator).
4. **Performance & Inclusivity**: Zero scroll-jacking, responsive layouts for all screen sizes, WCAG AA contrast compliance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target standard: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all public web surfaces.
- High-contrast typography (> 4.5:1 ratio for body text, > 3:1 for large display titles).
- Semantic HTML tags (`<main>`, `<nav>`, `<header>`, `<footer>`, `<section>`, `<article>`).
- Full keyboard navigability and touch-target minimums (≥ 44×44px on mobile).
